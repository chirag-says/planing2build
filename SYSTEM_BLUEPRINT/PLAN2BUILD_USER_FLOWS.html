Plan2Build → contractors

Standard quote request

Every contractor receives the same request: drawings, BOQ, specifications, timeline and a fixed quote format. Each line must be priced or marked as excluded, and staff can enter a quote for a contractor who does not use the portal. Questions go through Plan2Build; no contractor sees another's prices.

15

Plan2Build → homeowner

Scope-normalised comparison

Each quote is shown as submitted, with every difference from the specification (for example missing waterproofing or a lower steel grade) and its rupee impact, then comparable totals. The headline is never who is cheapest; the homeowner chooses.

16

Homeowner + contractor

Award the contract

The homeowner signs the construction contract directly with the chosen contractor and pays the contractor directly. Plan2Build is not a party to the contract; it records the agreed baseline.

17

Contractor + homeowner

Construction, stage by stage

The contractor builds through the 16 stages. The homeowner follows planned and actual dates, progress, upcoming decisions, documents and inspection evidence, while Plan2Build watches for overdue decisions, unanswered changes, open defects and stages behind plan.

Professionals / Service Providers

How professionals interact with Plan2Build

This shared flow applies to architects, contractors, interior designers and specialists. The structural engineer, the auditor, suppliers and brands work differently; their flows are in Section 3.

Join
1

Professional

Learn about Plan2Build

A professional reaches Plan2Build in one of three ways: an invitation to quote on a specific project, a local enrolment campaign that lists them in the public directory, or registering through the website's pages for professionals.

Decision to be confirmed

Which route, or mix of routes, applies is a final business decision.

Invited to one project

Invitation (contractors only)

The family nominates the contractor, or Plan2Build introduces one; the invitation arrives by project link and OTP. Continues in Section 3, Contractor, Path A.

How does the professional join?

Listed in the directory

Public directory listing

Basic listing is free and premium options exist. Homeowners view the profile and press Request Quote; what the professional receives after that is not yet designed.

Registers on the platform
2

Professional

Choose a category

Architect, civil contractor, interior designer or fit-out, or specialist (for example electrical, plumbing, waterproofing or solar), with the service area covered.

3

Professional

Build the profile and submit evidence

Identity, experience, portfolio and service area, plus registrations, licences, business and tax documents or insurance where the category asks for them.

4

Plan2Build

Verification review

The admin team checks the evidence against the rules for that category. A professional can be verified for one category and not yet for another.

Rejected

Cannot work as a verified professional. Whether they can apply again depends on Plan2Build's policy.

Review outcome?

Changes requested

The professional sees what to correct and resubmits; the review runs again (back to step 3).

Verified
5

Professional

Profile live

The profile can now appear to homeowners. Only verified professionals are eligible to be discovered.

If suspended

A policy breach or a lost credential suspends the account, or only the affected category: no new opportunities or quotes, existing records are kept, and Plan2Build later reinstates or closes it.

Win work
6

Plan2Build → professional

Receive matched opportunities

Plan2Build matches a project to eligible professionals by category, location, project type, budget, timing and availability, and alerts them. Professionals see project briefs, not a random lead feed.

7

Professional

View the opportunity

The brief shows location, budget band, scope, expected start and the service needed. The professional can save it and express interest.

Quote or decline?

Declines

The opportunity closes for this professional.

Quotes
8

Professional

Review the brief and clarify

Invited to quote by the homeowner or the system (or quoting straight from the opportunity), the professional opens the full brief and documents, such as the quote request and BOQ, and asks questions in the project conversation. A material change to the request creates a revised version.

9

Professional

Submit the quote

Price, line items, inclusions and exclusions, timeline, warranty, payment terms and validity, plus the fields of the professional's own category (Section 3). A submitted quote is never changed; a revision becomes a new version.

10

Homeowner

Compare and shortlist

The homeowner compares quotes mapped to a common scope, with exclusions and missing scope shown, and can shortlist without committing.

Selected?

No

Not selected: the opportunity closes for this professional.

Yes
Deliver
11

Professional

Accept the engagement

The professional confirms the selected quote and scope.

12

Plan2Build

Engagement activated

Work starts only once the agreed scope, verification, parties, payment schedule, required documents, start date and first milestone are in place. Selection alone never starts the work.

13

Professional

Deliver milestones with updates

Photos, videos, progress notes and documents are posted against each milestone. Progress is worked out from milestone weights; any percentage the professional proposes is stored next to it.

14

Professional + homeowner

Changes and issues

Scope changes go through change orders that the homeowner approves or rejects; nothing is applied silently. Issues the homeowner raises are fixed with proof, then verified and closed.

15

Homeowner + professional

Completion approval and payment

The professional requests completion of a milestone; the homeowner approves it or raises an issue.

Final business decision required
  • Payment through the platform, released to the professional after approval
  • Or payment directly between homeowner and professional, with Plan2Build only recording it
Close and grow
16

Professional + homeowner

Handover and warranty

Final documents, manuals, certificates and warranties are handed over and the snag list is closed. The homeowner accepts and the warranties are registered.

17

Homeowner → professional

Reputation and repeat work

Reputation comes from verified activity: projects completed, on-time delivery, response rate, profile strength, repeat clients and, where enabled, homeowner reviews. A strong record brings repeat opportunities.

Returns to step 6 for new work
