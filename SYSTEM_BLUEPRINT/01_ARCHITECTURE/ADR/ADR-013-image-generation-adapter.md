# ADR-013: Image generation behind an adapter; views are never authoritative; structural design never by AI

| Item | Value |
|---|---|
| Status | Proposed (2026-10-03); CD-25 by Chirag |
| Deciders | Chirag, Sakha |
| Related | IHB_FLOW.md 33.6, AI_AND_RECOMMENDATION_ARCHITECTURE.md Part A, INTEGRATION_ARCHITECTURE.md section 6 |

## Context

Chirag decided that Plan2Build provides a concept design by default with 3D views generated through image-generation APIs, and that architects provide better 2D or 3D designs on request. Image models produce pictures, not geometry; BOQ, estimate, RFQ and quotes must be measured from dimensioned drawings; structural design carries life-safety risk (BR-055). Hosted image models are retired often (Google withdrew Gemini 2.5 Flash Image on 2026-10-02).

## Decision

- Two artefact classes with a typed boundary: drawings (`is_authoritative = true`, produced by people or the later layout engine) and views (`VIEW_3D`, never authoritative, labelled "Illustrative; the drawings govern", generated by a provider). The BOQ reads only authoritative artefacts.
- One `ImageProvider` interface with `GeminiImageProvider` as the starting primary and `FluxKontextProvider` as the fallback, chosen by configuration after a one-week trial on real plots (AQ-15).
- Stage 1 guidance uses reference images (plan, elevation line drawing) plus a controlled style vocabulary; stage 2 adds depth and edge control images from an in-house 3D model.
- Every view is reviewed by the team against the plan before the homeowner sees it; at most two regeneration rounds; the Build Plan can issue without views.
- Providers receive geometry images with title blocks stripped and style words; never names, phones or addresses; data-use terms recorded.

## Why

- The typed boundary makes "a picture became a drawing of record" impossible rather than merely discouraged.
- The adapter makes model retirement a configuration change.
- Review keeps AI assistive (S06 §12) and protects homeowners from a convincing but wrong picture.
- Hosted APIs cost under ₹100 per project; self-hosting needs a GPU host that the volume does not justify.

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Why not |
|---|---|
| AI-generated floor plans as the drawings | Vendors themselves state their plans need professional review; dimensions are not exact; the BOQ would be wrong |
| One provider hard-coded | Retirement risk shown on 2026-10-02 |
| Self-hosted open models with ControlNet from day one | GPU cost and operations for a handful of images per week; planned as stage 2 if control inputs are needed |
| No 3D views at the POC | CD-25 asks for them; the cost is small and the boundary keeps the risk small |

## Consequences

- The team needs a reviewer with design judgement for views (CQ-26, AQ-16).
- Stage 2 requires the layout engine and a 3D model pipeline, which is product work beyond this blueprint.
- A canary job on staging catches provider changes weekly.

## Migration path

New adapters for new providers; control inputs added to `GenerateInput` without changing callers; self-hosting behind the same interface.
